# Legal notes (not legal advice)

1. **Patents.** DTS-HD (XLL/XBR/LBR) is patent-protected. The open `dcaenc` project carries a notice not to use it where software patents are enforced without a DTS licence. AC-3 patents expired March 2017; the last E-AC-3 patent was reported to expire January 2026. MLP/TrueHD and Atmos status is **unverified** — confirm before shipping native encoders.
2. **Trademarks.** Do not use DTS/Dolby logos or imply certification. Use "compatible with …".
3. **Specs and software.** Implement from public specs only: ETSI TS 102 114 (DTS), TS 102 366 (AC-3/E-AC-3), TS 103 190 (AC-4), Dolby's published TrueHD high-level bitstream description, and the multimedia.cx MLP notes. Do not copy code, text or images from proprietary tools or their (confidential) manuals.
4. **Atmos** has no public encoding spec; support is planned only through a user-supplied Dolby Encoding Engine backend.
